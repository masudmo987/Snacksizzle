Frequently Asked Questions

What areas do you deliver to?

We deliver across Johannesburg and surrounding areas. Check our delivery zones on the Contact page for specific details.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery typically takes 1-2 business days within Johannesburg, depending on your location and order volume. You’ll receive a tracking link once your order ships.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept Visa, Mastercard, EFT, and mobile payments like SnapScan. All transactions are secure and processed through trusted gateways.

Can I return or exchange a product?

Yes, if the product is unopened and within 7 days of delivery. Contact us via the Contact page with your order number for assistance.

Are your snacks fresh?

Absolutely! We source our snacks, including Simba Chips and Lunchbar, from local suppliers and ensure fast turnover to guarantee freshness.

Do you offer bulk orders or discounts?

Yes, we provide discounts for bulk orders. Reach out through the Contact page for a custom quote or check our Promotions page for current deals.

What should I do if my order is delayed or missing?

Contact our support team via the Contact page with your order details, and we’ll resolve it promptly—usually within 24 hours.

How can I track my order?

After dispatch, you’ll receive an email with a tracking link. Check your inbox (and spam folder) or log into your account on our site.

Are there minimum order requirements?

No minimum order is required, but orders over R200 qualify for free delivery within Johannesburg—see the Promotions page for details.

How do I contact customer support?

Use the Contact page to send us a message, or email us at support@snacksizzle.com. We aim to respond within 24 hours.
