5 Quick Snack Recipes with Simba Chips

Love Simba Chips? Try this simple Simba Nachos recipe!

Simba Nachos with Cheese and Salsa

  • 1 bag Simba Chips
  • 1 cup grated cheddar cheese (available at local stores)
  • 1/2 cup salsa (check your nearest supermarket)

Instructions: Preheat oven to 180°C. Spread Simba Chips on a baking tray, sprinkle with cheese, and bake for 5-7 minutes until melted. Top with salsa and enjoy!
